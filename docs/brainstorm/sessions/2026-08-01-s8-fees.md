## 🔴 Five verified defects

**1 · The list the founder asked for is already computed, and nothing asks for it.**
`GET /fees/overdue-students` returns student, class, overdue amount and earliest due date, sorted
oldest-first. `school-api.ts` has **no `overdueStudents` function**. The endpoint is live; the
screen never calls it.

> **Third time in eight sessions.** `S-86` (per-student homework history — built, wired into the
> client, called by nothing), `S-74` (the cover picker never reads the timesheet), now this. **The
> server keeps growing answers the client never asks for.** Worth a standing packet-close check:
> *does a screen call this?*

**2 · Arrears are invisible in every roll-up, so "pending" understates the truth.**
`student_fees.opening_dues` is excluded from `total_fee`, `collected_fee` and `overdue_amount`, and
the model says plainly that *"status is still driven by instalments only."* So **a student carrying
₹20,000 from last year with this year's instalments paid reads `paid`** and never appears on the
defaulter list. For a module whose entire purpose is *what is owed*, this is the one that matters
most (`Q-68`).

**3 · `/fees` leads with four bare numbers carrying three different denominators.**
`Net fee (year)` · `Collected` · `Overdue` · `Pending instalments` — two money, one money-past-a-
date, and one a **count of instalments** (not students, not families, not rupees). No sentence,
nothing named, nothing comparable. Exactly the shape DASH3-OV replaced on the dashboard eight days
ago; it survives here because nobody has opened this screen since P0-E.

**4 · `overdue_students` runs a query per student.** `_class_label` does `db.get(SchoolClass, …)`
inside the loop. Two hundred overdue students is two hundred round trips on a remote database, for
one list.

**5 · "Quarter" has no safe grouping key yet** — and the two obvious ones both break (below).

## The collision inside the founder's own decisions

> `D-63` — *"only admin should able to see the fee data"*
> `D-65` — *"assign teacher to followup with parents"*

**Both cannot be taken literally.** A task reading *"Call Kabir's father — ₹12,000 overdue"* puts
fee data on a teacher's phone, and the non-negotiable in `CLAUDE.md` and in `ux-principles.md`'s
hard-fence table is broken by the feature built on top of it.

`S-154` recommends **(a): the task names the family and the subject, never the amount.**

```
  Fee follow-up · Kabir Shah (8-B)
  Please speak to the family and record what they say.
  father Ramesh · 98xxx xxxxx              [ Call ]  [ Done ]
```

The parent already knows the number. She does not need it to make the call. And **the reason for
the fence is not confidentiality for its own sake — it is that a teacher who knows which families
are behind on fees treats those children differently**, and every child on that list has done
nothing wrong.

If (a) is taken, the inference routes close too: no sorting her tasks by amount, no *large/small*
badge, no count of how many families in her class are behind.

## Why a quarter is a due-date window

| Key | Breaks when |
|---|---|
| `installment_number` | structures differ — juniors on 2 instalments, seniors on 4, so *"instalment 1"* is a different quarter per class and the school-wide figure is nonsense |
| `label` | free text — *"Q1"*, *"Quarter 1"*, *"1st term"*, *"April"*; one school produces all four |
| **`due_date` inside a computed window** | ✅ works across differing structures, aligns every class to one calendar, needs no new column, and matches what the admin means: *"money due Jul–Sep"* |

An instalment with **no due date** is `unscheduled` — a word, not a bucket, never silently dropped
into Q1 (ux §10). `S-153`, `Q-67`.

## What Claude pushed that wasn't asked for

- **`S-152` — the board *is* `/fees`, not a ninth dashboard tab.** `/fees` exists and its landing
  page is the weakest screen in the product; the founder's "section in the dashboard" should
  replace it. A second fee screen would immediately disagree with the first — the `S-134` trap in
  a different module. **No new route, and `/fees/[id]` is untouched.**
- **`S-155` — lead with the shape.** *"₹42L of ₹68L"* is a fact nobody can act on. *"Q2 is 62%
  collected — ₹6.1L behind where Q1 stood in its fifth week"* is a decision. A collection curve
  with the previous quarter as a reference line, the same device `class-analytics` already uses.
- **`S-159` — every class row carries both denominators.** *"8-B — 14 of 38 families pending,
  ₹1.4L"*. One big defaulter and fourteen small ones need opposite actions, and a morning of phone
  calls is denominated in **calls**, not rupees — so the list sorts by families too.
- **`S-157` — a defaulter row is about a family's payment; it must never colour a child.** Name
  the guardian beside the student (they are who you ring), and **no fee status ever reaches an
  academic surface** — not `/students/[id]`, not growth, not the timeline, not the daily report,
  not a Lucy tool. A "fees pending" chip on a report card is one join away at all times and is the
  single most damaging thing this module could do.
- **`S-156` — money messages need manners.** One per instalment per week however many staff press
  the button; quiet hours; **stops the instant the payment lands**; `notify_opt_out` honoured.
  *A parent reminded three times in a morning about money will not read the fourth message about
  their child's attendance.*
- **`S-158` — a full concession is not a defaulter.** The arithmetic is already right; the risk is
  a list built from enrolment rather than from amount owed.
- **`S-161` — record the outcome, not the press.** *"Spoke to the mother — paying after the 15th"*
  is what makes the row go away. Without it the same family is rung every week by a different
  person, and the school looks disorganised to exactly the people it is asking for money.
- **`S-163` — three states, never two.** *Pending* (due later) and *overdue* (due, unpaid) are a
  forecast and a phone call. Never add them into one "outstanding" figure.

## What this module deliberately isn't

No second fee system (`D-62`) · no gateway and **no parent write** — `PC-1` is read-only and a
payment is the largest possible reversal of it · no fee status on academic screens · **no automatic
dunning** (money messages that fire themselves will eventually reach a family in the week of a
bereavement — same reasoning as the birthday wish, `S-129`) · no scholarship/concession workflow,
which is a different module and probably a different fence.
